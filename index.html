4C Clanker Code Cuck Chair

Every coding agent you run, in one terminal.

Force keeps your Claude Code and OpenCode sessions running on your machine or a server, whether a terminal is open or not, and tells you which one needs you.

$ curl -fsSL https://zalandemeter.github.io/install.sh | sh

Then type force. Linux, x86-64. No root, no packages, no config files.

One window. Every session.

The sidebar is the whole picture: seven sessions on one machine, one waiting for a permission, one finished since you last looked, two at work. This is Force itself, as text, not a screenshot.

Sit back. Force keeps watch.

◐ Sessions keep running
Close the terminal, log out, lose the connection: the agents carry on. A crashed one is restarted and picks up its conversation.
! You see who needs you
A permission prompt or a question in any session shows up in the sidebar and as a pill above the agent. One click takes you there.
● Any terminal
Force paints its own theme. GNOME Terminal, Konsole, kitty, Tilix, an ssh login: type force and it looks the same.
○ Several machines
Add a server with force host add; its sessions sit one click away from your laptop's.
● Your phone
Every session is reachable from the Claude app under the name it has in Force, renames included.
◌ The sessions you already have
force import lists your earlier conversations and takes over the one you pick, with its history.

The commands you will use.

forceOpen the cockpit. Ctrl+Space opens the menu inside it.
force new NAMEStart a session in the current folder.
force importList sessions Force doesn't manage yet; force import ID takes one over.
force host add NAMEAdd another machine you reach with ssh.
force settingsShow settings; force settings set KEY VALUE changes one.
force updateInstall the newest release. Sessions keep running.
force uninstallRemove Force; your conversations stay.
